kdrv
====

The chip drivers of the Kode Dot, one folder per part or group of parts. Only the AO that owns a part calls its driver.

Depends on: kdefs, klog, khal, kbsp

Docs: https://docs.kode.diy/sdk/kdrv/overview

Vendored: rfal/, RFAL 4.0.2 of STMicroelectronics, under SLA0051 (rfal/LICENSE.txt)
